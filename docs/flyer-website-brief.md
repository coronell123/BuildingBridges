# Building Bridges — website brief for flyers & pamphlets

Use this when designing a workshop flyer. Copy below is taken from the live website (`https://www.building-bridges.app`). Keep wording close; do not invent new project claims.

## 2. Who we are (short copy)

**English (homepage)**  
Mentoring & empowerment for girls and *FLINTA\* of Colour*.  
An interdisciplinary research and development project to empower girls and FLINTA\* of color from the 10th grade onwards to participate in higher education and academic careers in the psychosocial field.

**Deutsch (Startseite)**  
Mentoring & Empowerment für Mädchen und *FLINTA\* of Colour*.  
Ein interdisziplinäres Forschungs- und Entwicklungsprojekt zur Stärkung von Mädchen und FLINTA\* of Colour ab der 10. Klasse für Hochschule und akademische Laufbahnen im psychosozialen Bereich.

**Badge / kicker**  
EN: `Research Project 2024–2027 · Berlin · Duisburg-Essen`  
DE: `Forschungsprojekt 2024–2027 · Berlin · Duisburg-Essen`

**Footer tagline**  
EN: Empowering FLINTA individuals and girls of color to achieve their full potential in psychosocial and educational fields. 2024–2027.  
DE: Empowerment von FLINTA-Personen und Mädchen of Colour, damit sie ihr Potenzial in psychosozialen und Bildungsbereichen entfalten können. 2024–2027.

**Workshops page intro**  
EN: A storytelling-focused space for mentoring, empowerment, research exchange, and inclusive community building.  
DE: Mentoring, Empowerment, Forschungsaustausch und inklusives Community-Lernen — mit Storytelling im Mittelpunkt.

Always write **FLINTA\*** with the asterisk when space allows.

**Canva / InDesign paste**
```
#9152FF  #7339E0  #B580FF
#1A1033  #6B5F8A  #F2EEFF
#F5F0FF  #EDE5FF  #D7C3FF
#6BAA8A  #FFFFFF
```

**Gradients (same as website buttons)**  
`#9152FF` → `#7339E0` (CTA fill)  
`#9152FF` → `#B580FF` (accent bar)

**Do:** lavender background, ink headings, purple for “Building Bridges” and the main CTA.  
**Don’t:** the old sky-blue `#0EA5E9` / `#38BDF8`. That is not the public brand.

**Buttons on print**  
Filled CTA = `#9152FF` + white text. Outline = white fill, `#9152FF` border and text.

## 8. Suggested flyer building blocks

1. Purple kicker: `Forschungsprojekt 2024–2027`  
2. Title of *this* workshop (date, time, online/in person, location)  
3. One-line project description (section 2)  
4. Who it’s for (section 3)  
5. CTA: **Jetzt anmelden** / **Register now** + QR to `/sign-up` or the event form  
6. Website: **building-bridges.app**  
7. Partner + funding logos (small, footer)

For **online workshops**, also include: Zoom (or “link after registration”), duration, and language (DE/EN).

## 10. Notes for designers

- Site is bilingual **EN / DE**. Match the language of the audience.  
- Reproduce or distribute website content only with project permission (imprint copyright).  
- Existing workshop flyers live under `/workshops/updated/` on the site — use those as layout references, not as a second brand.  
- If the flyer is about **community stories / Globe / Video formats**, send people to `/stories` rather than inventing screenshots.
